Timeline

A brief history of the game of golf.

  • 1457

    The First

    The first known mention of golf in history. The Scottish Parliament banned golf because it distracted people from practicing archery!

  • 1744

    Growing the Game

    The first official golf club, The Honourable Company of Edinburgh Golfers, was formed in Scotland.

  • 1860

    Majors

    The first Open Championship (also known as the British Open) was played. It is the oldest golf tournament in the world.

  • 1997

    GOAT

    Tiger Woods became the youngest golfer (21 years old) to win the Masters Tournament, making history with a record-breaking score.
